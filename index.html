How it works

You keep your site and your passwords.

  1. You tell me what's wrong.Or I've written to you because I noticed something on your site.
  2. I reply with a fixed price.No work starts until you've agreed it.
  3. You invite me as an editor.I never ask for your password. An editor can correct pages but can't see your billing or delete the site. I'll send you the exact steps for your platform.
  4. I make the change and you check it.Then you remove my access. It takes about ten seconds.

For committees and trustees: sharing one password between people is what charity guidance tells you not to do. NCVO's cybersecurity guidance says to give each person their own login with the least access they need. An editor invitation does exactly that.
